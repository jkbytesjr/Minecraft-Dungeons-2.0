# Pixel Bastion 1.0

The first full release of Pixel Bastion, a voxel dungeon crawler that runs in your browser. The project was called Voxel Dungeon until this release.

Fight down through endless procedurally generated floors, each one a different world. The game has four bosses, loot with special powers, and level-up choices that shape your build. Everything you see and hear is generated in code: there are no image or audio files in the game.

## Highlights

**A remastered world.** A fixed 45° isometric camera looks down on open arenas joined by wide corridors. Rooms have raised altars, stepped ledges and sunken pits connected by block staircases. They stand on layered stone cliffs and floating islands above deep chasms.

**Six biomes, never the same twice.** Each floor is one of six biomes:

- Ancient Temple
- Overgrown Ruins
- Dark Keep
- Sky Bastion
- Crystal Catacombs
- Garden Courtyard

Each biome is lit in a mood such as day, dusk, mist, torchlight, arcane or ember. Back-to-back floors never share a biome, and no two floors in a run look alike.

**Built block by block.** The world is built from small blocks, not flat surfaces:

- Walls are individual bricks and stones, topped with cornices and battlements.
- Doorways have arches, and walls have chiselled pillars and alcoves with iron gates.
- Floors blend cobblestone, slate, sandstone and terracotta, with inlaid mosaics and worn edges.
- Room corners hold crates, barrels, urns and planters.

**Light and atmosphere.** Torches flicker in iron sconces. Mana crystals, magic circles and water channels glow and cast coloured light. The sun casts long shadows, and bloom and haze settle over the depths.

## What's new

### World

- The fixed isometric camera, the six biomes and the lighting moods described above.
- Rooms are bigger and corridors are 5 to 7 tiles wide, so fights have room to breathe. Enemy numbers are scaled so the difficulty stays the same.
- Raised and sunken floor sections. Characters walk up and down the steps smoothly.

### Gameplay

- **Tutorial:** a six-room walkthrough of moving, fighting, dodging, abilities, loot and a boss. New runs offer it first.
- **Level-ups wait for a lull.** The attribute choice appears once you're out of combat, so it never interrupts a fight. Press L to choose straight away.
- **Spear volley:** the E ability now throws a fan of seven spears.
- **New enemies:** spider packs, shieldbearers that block attacks from the front, shamans that heal their allies, and wraiths that blink behind you. There are also elite versions of every monster.
- **Saves:** continue a run later from the title screen. The game autosaves at the start of every floor.

### Character

- **Character creator:** choose your hero's skin, hair style and colour, beard, eyes and clothing, with a live preview.
- Your equipped armor and weapon show on your character, including capes that swing as you run.
- Improved animation throughout: attack wind-ups and follow-through, hit flinches, squash and stretch, and bodies that topple when they die.

### Mods

- Drop JSON mod files into `public/mods/` (or import them from the title screen) to change the rules and add new items and enemy variants.
- `public/mods/example-mod.json` shows every option. The full format is in [`public/mods/README.md`](../../public/mods/README.md).

### Fixes

- Armor no longer clips or flickers. Helmets follow the head, and capes and hair no longer poke through other pieces.
- Movement no longer jitters at very high speeds or attack speeds, and walking toward the mouse eases to a stop at the cursor.

## Play

Play it in your browser at the GitHub Pages link on the repository page. To run it yourself:

```sh
npm install
npm run dev
```

Then open http://localhost:5173.
